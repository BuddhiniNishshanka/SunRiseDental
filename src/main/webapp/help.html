Staff Operational Help & Standard Procedures

Step-by-step user manual and technical guidance for clinic staff

Return to Dashboard

1. Staff Authentication & System Access

To access the Sunrise Dental Management System, each authorized staff member must log in with their allocated credentials.

  1. Navigate to the login screen (index.html).
  2. Enter your designated Staff Username and Password.
  3. Upon successful authentication, a secure session cookie (JSESSIONID) is initiated with role-based permissions (Admin, Receptionist, Dentist).

2. How to Register a New Patient Appointment

Follow these steps to schedule a patient consultation or dental procedure:

  1. Click Register Appointment from the sidebar navigation.
  2. Enter the patient's full name, address, and valid contact number (10-digit format).
  3. Select the appropriate Dentist from the dropdown menu (consultation fee and specialization will update automatically).
  4. Select the desired Treatment Type (e.g. Scaling, Filling, Root Canal).
  5. Pick the Appointment Date and available Time Slot.
    💡 Note: The system contains built-in conflict detection (MySQL database trigger & service validator) that automatically prevents double-booking identical doctor slots.
  6. Click Confirm & Register Appointment. The system will generate a unique Appointment Number (e.g. APP-2026-005) and dispatch simulated SMS and Email alerts.

3. Searching and Inspecting Appointment Details

When a patient arrives at the clinic or calls the front desk:

  1. Open the Search Appointments module.
  2. Type the unique Appointment Number (e.g. APP-2026-001) or the patient's name/contact number into the search bar.
  3. The system displays full details including patient contact information, assigned dentist, scheduled time slot, and current status.
  4. Staff can view complete appointment records, initiate cancellations if necessary, or proceed directly to billing.

4. Billing Calculation, Discounts & Receipt Printing

When the dental treatment is completed:

  1. Navigate to the Calculate & Print Bill screen.
  2. Select the patient's appointment. The system automatically computes the Consultation Fee + Treatment Standard Fee.
  3. Apply promotional discount percentages or tax rates if applicable. The system applies dynamic Strategy Pattern rules (e.g., automated 10% Senior Citizen subsidies).
  4. Select the payment method (Cash, Card, Transfer, Insurance) and click Settle Payment & Generate Official Invoice.
  5. Click View & Print Official Patient Receipt to open the printable receipt formatted with clinic credentials and itemized fee breakdown.

5. Safe System Exit (Logout)

To safely terminate your active session:

  • Click the red Logout button at the bottom of the sidebar.
  • Your session cookie is invalidated on the server, ensuring privacy and compliance with data protection policies.
